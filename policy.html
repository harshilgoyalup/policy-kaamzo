Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Kaamzo. This Privacy Policy explains how Kaamzo collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Kaamzo mobile application, website, and related services.

By using Kaamzo, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Kaamzo, we may collect:

  • Name and basic profile information.
  • Email address.
  • Mobile phone number.
  • Account authentication information.
  • Worker profile and professional information.
  • Work category and skills.
  • Location information when required for service matching.
  • Information submitted while creating or accepting jobs.
  • Device and application information.
  • Information required to maintain security and prevent misuse.

3. Aadhaar Information

If Aadhaar information is requested for worker verification, it will only be collected and processed for the stated verification purpose and in accordance with applicable laws and requirements.

Important: Users should only provide information through the official Kaamzo application or authorized verification process.

4. Google Sign-In

Kaamzo may allow users to create or access an account using Google Sign-In. When you use Google Sign-In, we may receive information such as your name, email address, and profile information made available through the authentication service.

5. Phone Number and OTP

Kaamzo may use phone-number authentication and one-time passwords (OTP) to verify users and protect accounts.

Your phone number may also be used for account-related communication, security notifications, and service-related purposes.

6. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users.
  • Connect customers with workers.
  • Provide job and service matching.
  • Process and manage jobs.
  • Improve the Kaamzo platform.
  • Maintain security and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Send important service and account communications.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

7. Location Information

Kaamzo may request location information to help customers and workers discover or match with nearby services.

Location access can generally be controlled through your device settings. Some features may not function correctly if location access is disabled.

8. Data Storage and Security

Kaamzo uses appropriate technical and organizational measures designed to protect user information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee complete security of information.

9. Third-Party Services

Kaamzo may use trusted third-party services to provide authentication, database, hosting, analytics, communication, verification, or other technical functionality.

These providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services and according to their applicable privacy policies and agreements.

10. Data Sharing

Kaamzo does not sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be shared when necessary to provide Kaamzo services, protect users, operate the platform, comply with legal obligations, or prevent fraud and misuse.

11. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

When information is no longer required, it may be deleted, anonymized, or securely disposed of where appropriate.

12. Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their Kaamzo account and associated personal information, subject to information that we may be required to retain by law or for legitimate security and operational purposes.

13. Children's Privacy

Kaamzo is not intended for children who are below the minimum age required to independently use the service under applicable law.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to address the situation.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page and the updated date will be modified accordingly.

15. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the Kaamzo team.
